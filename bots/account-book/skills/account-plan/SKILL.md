# Account plan

Part of the Account Research Desk agent preset.

Inputs: the account file with its brief, stakeholder map, signals log, usage numbers, and open threads, plus what the user sells.

1. Ask two things when the file does not already answer them: what a win here looks like, and by when. Skip both when you know.
2. Write the plan in seven short parts.
   - Where this stands: stage in their words, last touch, and what is true today.
   - Why they would buy: the problem in the account's own words when you have them, and the evidence, sourced.
   - Who decides: champion, economic buyer, evaluators, blockers, and the gaps.
   - What is in the way: the real risks, each with what would prove it or kill it. No optimism padding.
   - The path: the next three meetings worth having and what each one has to produce.
   - Next 30, 60, 90: at most three actions per block, each with an owner and a date.
   - What would change this plan: two or three signals to watch for.
3. Mark every assumption as an assumption. A plan built on three guesses should say so at the top.
4. Show it in chat, take one round of edits, then save it.

Output: the plan above, under a page. Offer to put it in Notion or post a summary to Slack when either is connected, as a draft they approve first.

Fallbacks: an account with no notes yet gets a plan built from public research with every gap written as a question to ask on the next call, and you say plainly that it is research only.

Update the plan whenever the signals check finds something material or a call closes threads, and say what you changed.

Save the plan into the account file.

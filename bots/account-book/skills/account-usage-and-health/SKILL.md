# Account usage and health

Part of the Account Research Desk agent preset.

Inputs: numbers from a warehouse query through Hex, a CSV or dashboard export they paste or upload, support or ticket counts, or their own read of the account. This skill never guesses at usage.

1. Say where the numbers came from and what date range they cover, in the first line. A number with no source and no date does not go in.
2. When you do not know which measures matter, ask once and offer the usual ones: active users against licensed seats, the trend over the last 90 days, which parts of the product get used, support or ticket volume, and anything still open on their side.
3. Compare against the last time you looked, when the account file has it. Direction matters more than the absolute number.
4. Call health green, amber, or red, and name the single number that drives the call. One reason, not a paragraph.
5. Write three risks and three openings to grow, each with the evidence next to it and the person who would care.
6. Point at what this changes in the account plan and in the open threads.

Output: the numbers table with source and date, the trend read, the health call with its one reason, then risks and openings.

Fallbacks: no data at all means you say so plainly, then ask five questions that get you a usable picture from what they know. Never estimate seats, revenue, or usage, and never present their own guess back to them as a measurement.

Save the numbers and the health call, dated, into the account file.

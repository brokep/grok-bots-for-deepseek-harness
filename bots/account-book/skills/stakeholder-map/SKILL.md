# Stakeholder map

Part of the Account Research Desk agent preset.

Inputs: the company name and site, the names already in the user's notes, and anything they paste such as an email thread, an attendee list, or an org chart.

1. Start from the names the user already has. Someone in their notes matters more than anyone you find cold.
2. For each person, find title, team, tenure, and one public signal such as a talk, a post, a job change, or something they built. Source every one.
3. Fill gaps by role rather than by guess: who runs the function that would own this purchase, who holds the budget above them, and who does the evaluating.
4. Tag each person champion, economic buyer, technical evaluator, blocker, or unknown. Use unknown unless the user or a source told you otherwise.
5. Say the gaps out loud: which roles you have nobody for, and which named person you know nothing about.

Output: a table with name, title, team, role in this deal, how you know, and source. Then three lines: who is missing, who to meet next and why, and one warm path in when a public connection exists.

Fallbacks: a person with no public footprint gets "not findable publicly" written in their row. Never guess a title, never take a reporting line from a rumor or an org chart you cannot see, and never collect a personal email address or phone number.

Save the map into the account file and re-run it when a new name shows up in their notes.

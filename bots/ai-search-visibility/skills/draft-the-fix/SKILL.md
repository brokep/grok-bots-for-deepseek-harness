# Draft the fix

Part of the AI Search Visibility agent preset.

Inputs: the fix list item, the prompts it should move, the capture showing what the answers that beat them say, and their site.

1. Name the target in one line: a page on their site, a listing on a third-party source, or a profile they can claim, with the exact URL.
2. Read the answers that beat them and quote two or three of those sentences. Write to what the answers reward, not to a keyword.
3. Read two or three pages of their site first and match that voice. A draft that does not sound like them gets rewritten and never shipped.
4. Shape it by target. A listing gets the fields that source asks for, in their order. A page gets a title, the buyer's question as a heading, a direct answer in the first two lines, then the detail. A profile gets the fields plus one line on what makes them different.
5. Put in the specifics that get cited: named numbers, named integrations, plain pricing, who it is for and who it is not for. Answers cite pages that state facts. Never invent a number, ask for anything you are missing.
6. Hand it over as a draft with the URL to submit to or the page to update. Never submit, post, or publish it yourself.
7. Close with which prompts this should move and when you will check, usually the next weekly brief after it goes live.

Fallbacks: no public submission path means say so and pick the next item. No answer yet quotes anyone on this prompt, so write the plainest correct answer to the question and say that is the bet.

Save the draft under the visibility folder and attach it.

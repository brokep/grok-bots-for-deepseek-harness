# Weekly ad review

Part of the Apple Search Ads Review agent preset.

Inputs: the exports covering the last 7 to 14 days, the targets sheet, and the previous weekly reviews.

1. Open with one line: the date range, total spend, total installs, the blended cost per install, and the gap to target.
2. Trend. Spend and cost per install week over week per campaign, which way each is moving, and whether the move is bigger than the normal week to week swing. Split cost per tap and conversion rate so the cause is visible, not just the total.
3. Where the money went wide. The three or four groups costing the most money above target, each with the money figure and one sentence on the likely cause: a bid raise, a budget increase with no headroom, a new keyword set, Search Match drifting, seasonality, or a creative set that stopped working.
4. Where there is room. Groups under target with volume and unspent budget, and what you would move into them. Flag anything that hit its daily cap most days.
5. Keywords. This week's harvest and negative candidates from the search terms and negatives skill, and the bid changes from bids and budgets.
6. Last week's list. Which recommendations they applied, what happened after, and which are still open.
7. Next week. Three to five changes in priority order, each with the number behind it.

Rules: no recommendation without a number from the export. Say when a week is too thin to read. A quiet week is three lines, not a full review. Never claim a change caused a result when a budget shift, a seasonal swing, or an App Store change explains it just as well.

Save the dated review under the Apple Search Ads folder and attach it, then deliver it to the destination they picked. Anything that posts or emails goes to them as a draft first.

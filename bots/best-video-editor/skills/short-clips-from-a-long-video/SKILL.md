# Short clips from a long video

Part of the Video Edit Desk agent preset.

Inputs: a long file with a transcript and a shot map, plus the platform and the length they want.

1. Read the transcript and the shot map first. Never propose a clip from a stretch of the file you have not read.
2. Propose five candidates. Each one gets a start and end timecode, the opening line quoted exactly from the transcript, one clause on why it stands alone, and the runtime.
3. Say what you picked on: a complete thought, a question and its answer, a number or a claim, a visible demo, a clean audio span. You are reading what is in the file. Which clip is worth posting is their call.
4. They pick. Then cut each one, reframe it to the aspect in memory, add captions if captions are on, and trim the dead air off the head and tail.
5. Name every clip so the moment is findable again: a plain label plus its start timecode in the source.
6. Deliver the clips together with a one-line index: clip, runtime, and the quoted hook.

Fallbacks: no transcript means you make one first, it takes a minute and everything else depends on it. A file with no speech gets candidates off the shot map and the stills instead, and you say the picks are visual. A moment that runs past the platform limit gets shown at both lengths rather than quietly trimmed.

Never claim a clip will perform or go viral. Never stitch words from two parts of the video into one sentence, and never move a quote out of the context that makes it true.

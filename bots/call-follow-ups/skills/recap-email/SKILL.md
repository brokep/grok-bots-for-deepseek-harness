# recap-email

## When to use

A call finished (from `new-call-watch`) or the owner asks for a follow-up ("draft the recap for my Acme call"). Input: a call id from Gong or Granola, an account name plus date, or a pasted transcript.

## Read the call

1. Pull the summary and the full transcript from whichever recorder has the call. Gong and Granola are equal inputs here; use Granola notes for the meetings Gong did not record.
2. Identify: who attended, what the customer said their problem and priority are (exact words), decisions made, questions left open, every commitment made by either side, resources the customer asked for, and the agreed next meeting or milestone.
3. Pull the customer's own words for the one line of recap. Never attribute a preference or opinion to a customer unless the transcript contains it.

## Draft

Follow the owner's voice profile (greeting, sign-off, sentence length, signature block). Structure, in order:

1. Greeting line, always. Never start with the body.
2. One line of recap in the customer's framing.
3. Next steps as a short list with an owner and a date on each. Ours first, then theirs.
4. Resources: only the links or documents that were explicitly agreed on the call, one line each. Do not add unrequested collateral.
5. Access or trial details if any were promised, with who needs to do what.
6. Close with the agreed next meeting, or one concrete proposal for it, and the sign-off.

Length: under 150 words in the body. Short paragraphs, whitespace between them. No feature lists, no walls of text.

## Deliver

Save the email in Drafts addressed to the external attendees who were actually on the call, cc'ing internal attendees, subject `Follow-up: <topic> (<date>)` unless the voice profile says otherwise. Show the owner the draft with a one-line note on anything you were unsure about. Ask before making any change to recipients.

## Rules

- Drafts only. Never send. Never reply into an existing thread without the owner's yes.
- No em dashes. No hyperbole or superlatives. Plain, specific prose.
- No metrics, logos, or proof points that were not said on the call or supplied by the owner.
- If the transcript is missing or under two minutes, say so and offer to draft from the owner's notes instead.

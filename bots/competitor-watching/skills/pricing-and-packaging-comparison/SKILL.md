# Pricing and packaging comparison

Part of the Competitor Watch agent preset.

Inputs: the pricing pages on the watch list, and the user's own pricing page when they gave it.

1. Fetch each public pricing page and note the fetch date.
2. Pull per plan: plan name, price, billing period, what the price is charged per (a seat, a team, or a usage unit), any seat or spend minimum, the limits that bite such as usage caps, feature gates, and support tier, and whatever is free.
3. Build one table. Keep the wording each company uses for its own plans instead of normalizing it away.
4. Write call for pricing exactly as that when a price sits behind a sales conversation. Never estimate it.
5. Add three to five lines under the table: where the user is cheapest, where they are most expensive, which limit is most likely to come up in a deal, and what moved since the last comparison when you have one.

Fallbacks: enterprise-only pricing gets a note, not a guess. A currency or region difference gets stated. A pricing page behind a login is out of scope.

Save the comparison dated under competitors with the page dates inside it, then attach it.

# Add sources to the proof bank

Part of the Customer Proof Desk agent preset.

Inputs: a pasted transcript, call notes, a recording summary, a support thread, an NPS or survey reply, a customer email, a public review, or a link to any of those.

1. Read the source and say what it is, who is in it, which company they are from, and the date. Ask for the date and the speaker names when they are missing. Never guess either one.
2. Pull every candidate quote word for word. Keep the speaker's own wording, including the awkward parts. Never rewrite inside quote marks. A cut for length is marked with an ellipsis. A word added for context goes in square brackets. Those are the only two edits allowed.
3. Record per quote: the exact text, who said it with their title and company, the source and its date, and the claim it supports.
4. Record numbers separately: the metric, the value, the time period, how it was measured if they said, and the same source and date. A number with no time period is incomplete. Flag it rather than dropping it.
5. Set the approval state on every new item to not requested.
6. Write the rows into the proof bank, then say how many quotes and numbers you added and what you flagged.

Output: the quotes and numbers in chat with speaker and date on every line, the count filed, and the gaps worth chasing on the next call.

Fallbacks: an audio or video file you cannot read means you ask for the transcript text, or the export from their call recorder. A source with no named speaker still gets filed, tagged unattributed, and can only ever be used anonymized. Someone summarizing what a customer said is not a quote. File it as background.

Save under the proof bank folder.

# Get the customer's approval

Part of the Customer Proof Desk agent preset.

Inputs: the exact items to be approved, where they will appear, and who the contact is.

1. You write the request. You never send it. Hand them the draft.
2. Keep it to five sentences: what you would like to publish, the exact quote and number in full so there is no ambiguity, where it will appear, that they can edit or decline any line, and the date you would like an answer by.
3. List each item separately with a yes or no beside it, so they can approve one and refuse another. Five separate yeses are a stronger record than one yes to a block of five.
4. Ask who else needs to see it when the company runs quotes through legal, comms, or a manager, and address the request to that group.
5. Offer the anonymized fallback in the same message. Plenty of customers who cannot approve their name will approve "a Series B fintech" carrying the same numbers.
6. Record the outcome in the bank the moment they tell you: approved for public use, approved anonymized only, approved with an edit (store the edited wording as the new quote text), or refused (mark it never publish and keep it out of every future pull).

Output: the request ready to copy, plus one line on what changes in the bank once they hear back. When Gmail is connected, save it as an unsent draft and say it is sitting there.

Follow-ups: one nudge after a week, one more after two, then mark it stalled and stop. Never chase a customer harder than that.

Save under the proof bank folder.

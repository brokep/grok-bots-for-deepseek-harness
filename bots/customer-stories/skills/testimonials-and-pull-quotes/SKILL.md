# Testimonials and pull quotes

Part of the Customer Proof Desk agent preset.

Inputs: a source with the customer speaking, or rows already in the bank.

1. Start from the exact words. Every option you produce is a subset of what the person actually said.
2. Give three lengths from the same quote: a pull quote under 15 words, a two sentence testimonial, and the long version with the setup. Show the untouched original underneath all three so they can see exactly what you cut.
3. Cuts are marked with an ellipsis. Words added for context go in square brackets. Nothing else changes, not the grammar, not the word order, not a stray "like".
4. Add the attribution line the destination needs: name, title, and company for a site, first name and role for the anonymized version, company alone when the person prefers that.
5. Say where each option is strongest. A landing page wants the punchy line, a deck wants the one with the number in it, a review site wants the specific detail.
6. Mark anything not approved for public use, and offer to draft the approval request in the same message.

Output: the three options, the original underneath, the attribution line, and the approval state.

Fallbacks: a source that is someone else summarizing the customer is not quotable. It can support a claim in your own words, it cannot go inside quote marks. Say that plainly rather than tidying it into a quote.

Save under the proof bank folder.

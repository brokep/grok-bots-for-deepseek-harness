# three-whys

## When to use

A first or second meeting with a prospect, an executive intro, or a champion who needs a deck to carry internally. "Three whys for Acme", "update the Acme deck with what we learned Tuesday".

## Create mode

1. Research: the prospect's stated strategy and initiatives (their own statements first), the industry pressure they face, and any prior conversation in Gong or the CRM. Quotes from their leadership and from our calls are the strongest material.
2. Slide 1, Why change: the specific tension in their business, in their words where possible, with the cost of staying put stated by them or by a credible public source.
3. Slide 2, Why now: the event or signal that makes this quarter the time (a program in flight, a deadline, a competitor move, a hiring wave).
4. Slide 3, Why us: the outcome we produce against that tension, one approved proof point from a comparable company, and how it would look for them. Third person throughout, no first-person hype.
5. Slide 4, Proposed path: two or three steps to a decision with dates.

Speaker notes carry the source of every claim.

## Update mode

When the owner adds new context ("they told us budget opens in Q1", "the CTO cares about security review"), change only the slides that context touches, keep the rest, and publish a new version of the file with a one-line change note.

## Build

Brand from `brand-kit`, pptx through `pptx-compat`, delivered as a file. Upload only to a folder the owner named.

## Rules

- Only approved proof points and customer names. Nothing from earlier drafts counts as a source.
- Never sends. The owner shares the deck.
- No em dashes, no superlatives, one idea per slide.

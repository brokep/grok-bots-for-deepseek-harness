# Build the recap deck

Part of the Meeting Recap Deck agent preset.

Inputs: the meeting notes in any form, the template profile, and who the recap is going to.

1. Say what you are working from in one line: the meeting, the date, and where the notes came from. Ask for the company name and the date when the notes do not carry them.
2. Pull three things out of the notes and nothing else: what they said, in their problems, their words, and their asks, what anyone committed to, and the themes that came up more than once.
3. What we heard. Three cards is the usual shape. Each card is their problem in their language rather than your read on it. Quote them when the quote is short and clear. No card without a line in the notes behind it.
4. Next steps. One line each: what, who owns it, and by when. Keep both sides, theirs and yours. A commitment with no owner or no date gets written that way and flagged, never filled in with a guess.
5. Themes. Only when the notes carry more than one meeting's worth of signal, or when they asked for it. A rollup across several meetings is a different job, that one goes to Themes across meetings.
6. Carry over any next step from an earlier recap with the same company that is past its date, as one short line at the end.
7. Fit the shape in the template profile. Cut to fit the card rather than shrinking the type. Anything that will not fit goes in a short list under the deck instead.
8. Show the full text of every slide in chat first, then build the deck file. Never make them open a file to find out whether you got it right.
9. Close with what you would do next in one line: send it, ticket the next steps, or fix a card.

Rules: every line traces to the notes. No invented quotes, numbers, names, dates, or commitments. Say which parts are thin when the notes are thin. Your own read on the deal or the relationship stays out, this one goes to the other side of the table.

Output: the slide text in chat, the deck file attached, and one line on what you left out and why.

Save the recap dated under the recaps folder with the notes it came from.

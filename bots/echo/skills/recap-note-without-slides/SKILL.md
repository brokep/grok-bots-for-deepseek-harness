# Recap note without slides

Part of the Meeting Recap Deck agent preset.

Inputs: the meeting notes, and who is going to read it.

1. Ask one thing when it is not already obvious: is this going to the customer, or to their own team. The two read differently and everything else follows from the answer.
2. Write it short. A subject line with the company and the date, two or three sentences on what you heard, the next steps as a list with an owner and a date on each, and one line on when they will hear back.
3. Use their words for their problems. No summary of the summary, no restating the agenda, no thanks for the time.
4. Keep it under 200 words unless they ask for more, or unless memory holds a different length they picked. A recap that reads like a memo does not get read.
5. A version for their own team can carry the open questions and what is still unclear. A version for the customer carries only what was said and what was agreed.
6. Hand it back as text they can copy. When Gmail, Notion, or Drive is connected, offer to save it as an unsent draft, a page, or a doc, and say plainly that it is sitting there unsent.

Rules: same as the deck. Nothing in the note that is not in the notes. No invented quotes, numbers, or commitments.

Output: the note in chat, ready to copy.

Save it dated under the recaps folder next to the meeting it came from.

# Share the approved recap

Part of the Meeting Recap Deck agent preset.

Inputs: the recap they approved, and where it goes.

1. Nothing leaves until they say yes to that exact version. A "looks good" on a draft is not a yes to sending it.
2. Ask one question when the destination is not obvious: who reads it, and by what route.
3. Slack: post the recap in the channel they name with the deck attached, short enough to read without expanding. Show the message text first.
4. Gmail: save it as an unsent draft with the subject line, the note body, and the deck attached, then say plainly that it is sitting in drafts and nobody has it yet.
5. Notion or Google Drive: save it where they keep the others, name it with the company and the date, and hand back the link.
6. Nothing connected is fine. Hand back the text and the file shaped for the route they picked, so they can send it in one paste.
7. Write down that it went out, the date, and who it went to, so the Friday roundup can tell sent from still sitting.

Rules: the version that goes out is the version they approved, word for word. No last-minute edits on the way out the door. Never add a recipient they did not name, and never send to the other side of the table on a routine.

Output: the exact text you are about to send, then the confirmation once they say go.

Save the sent record next to the dated recap.

# Themes across meetings

Part of the Meeting Recap Deck agent preset.

Inputs: which company or which stretch of time, and the recaps you already have.

1. Read every dated recap that matches. Say how many you found and the range of dates before you write anything.
2. A theme is something that showed up in at least two meetings. Name how many times and which meetings. One mention is a detail, not a theme.
3. Write each theme in their words, then one line on how it moved: first raised, still open, resolved, or getting louder.
4. Next steps across the set: what got done, what is past its date, and what quietly disappeared. The disappeared ones are usually the useful part.
5. Say what you cannot see. Meetings with no notes, gaps of several weeks, and anything you are reading from a single recap all get named out loud.
6. Output follows the template profile, one block per theme, or a short written rollup when they want something to send.
7. Never merge two companies into one rollup, and never move a quote from one meeting into a theme it does not belong to.

Fallback: fewer than two recaps means say so and offer to build the missing ones from notes they paste.

Output: the theme list in chat, then the deck or the note.

Save the rollup dated under the recaps folder.

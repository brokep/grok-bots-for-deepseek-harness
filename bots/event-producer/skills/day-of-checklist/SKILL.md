# Day-of checklist

Part of the Event Producer agent preset.

Inputs: the run of show, the vendor tracker, the guest list, and the guest travel sheet.

1. Build the checklist in five blocks: before you leave, load-in and setup, doors and program, during, and tear-down.
2. Before you leave: printed run of show, printed guest list with dietary and access marked, name badges, signage, cables and adapters, chargers, a payment card on hand, water, and the phone number of every vendor arriving that day.
3. Load-in and setup: vendor arrival times pulled straight from the tracker, room layout, an AV check with the actual laptop and the actual slides, sound check, wifi tested, signage placed, seating or place cards set from the guest list, and the check-in table ready.
4. Doors and program: who is on the door, how walk-ins are handled, how a plus-one gets added on the spot, who welcomes the guests arriving from out of town, who does the opening, the cue lines from the run of show, and who calls the timing.
5. During: the three things to watch. Usually food timing, drink levels, and the room getting too loud for the program. Add the dietary plates and who is delivering them to the right seats.
6. Tear-down: what leaves with whom, what the venue keeps, lost property, and the final walk-through.
7. Add a short what-if list: speaker no-show, AV dies, catering late, a dietary plate missing, twice the expected headcount, half the expected headcount. One line each on what you do.
8. Put a phone number next to every name.

Output: a printable checklist with boxes, ordered by time, plus the what-if list and a one-page contact sheet.

Fallbacks: an unconfirmed vendor gets a line that says unconfirmed and a chase draft alongside it.

Save under the event folder as the day-of checklist.

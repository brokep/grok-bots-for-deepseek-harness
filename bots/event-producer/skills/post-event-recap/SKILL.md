# Post-event recap

Part of the Event Producer agent preset.

Inputs: the guest list, the vendor tracker, the guest travel sheet, the measures set in the brief, and whatever the user tells you about how it went.

1. Ask for the three things you cannot know: actual attendance, anything spent that never hit the tracker, and how the room felt. Nothing else.
2. Open with the outcome in one line. What the event was for, and whether it hit the measures in the brief.
3. The numbers: invited, registered, attended, no shows, show rate, and cost per attendee. Compare the show rate to the assumption you planned with and say what to assume next time.
4. Money: quoted against actual by category, and the total against budget. Name what came in over.
5. What worked and what did not. Three to five lines each, specific enough to act on. A late vendor gets named. A room that was too small gets a number. A dietary plate that never arrived gets written down.
6. Follow-ups: every person who needs a message and what it says. Thanks to speakers and vendors, replies to attendees who asked for something, and one line to the people who registered and did not come. Skip anyone who opted out.
7. Do it again: what to book earlier, what to cut, what to keep.

Output: the recap, plus the follow-up drafts ready to copy.

Fallbacks: no attendance number means you run the recap on registrations and mark every figure that depends on it. Never send a thank you, a follow-up, or a survey without a yes.

Save under the event folder as the dated recap, and carry the show rate, the dietary counts, and the vendor notes forward so the next event starts smarter.

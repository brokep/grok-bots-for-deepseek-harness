# Vendor and venue tracker

Part of the Event Producer agent preset.

Inputs: quotes, emails, a pasted list, a spreadsheet, or nothing but the categories this event has to fill.

1. One row per vendor: category, company, contact name, contact email or phone, what they provide, quoted amount, status, deposit due date, balance due date, arrival time on the day, and notes.
2. Status is one of quoted, held, contracted, deposit paid, confirmed, or cancelled. Never write confirmed unless the user says it is confirmed or you have the confirmation in front of you.
3. For the venue, also capture load-in access, capacity, what is included, what costs extra, the noise or end-time limit, step-free access and accessible bathrooms, insurance requirements, and the cancellation terms. The last few are what wreck events.
4. Total the quoted amounts and hold them against the budget in the brief. Say the number, and name the categories with no quote yet.
5. Take the dietary and access counts from the guest list and give catering and the venue real numbers rather than a guess. Restate them every time the count moves.
6. Flag every row still unconfirmed inside two weeks of the event, and every deposit due in the next seven days.
7. Draft the chase message for anything stalled. Three to five sentences, opens with the event name and date, states what you are waiting on, and names the date you need an answer by.

Output: the tracker table, the money line, the flagged rows, and any drafts ready to copy.

Fallbacks: no quotes yet means you build the empty tracker with the categories this event type needs, so the gaps are visible. Never send vendor mail. When Gmail is connected, save it as a draft in their account and say it is sitting there unsent.

Save under the event folder as the vendor tracker.

# Answer a spend question

Part of the Ad Spend Watch agent preset.

Inputs: the numbers ledger and the question.

Steps:
1. Answer in the first line with the number. No preamble.
2. Second line: the date range you used and the freshest date in the ledger.
3. One line of context only when it changes what they would do, such as the trend against the prior period or the fact that the campaign sits under the quiet floor.
4. When the ledger does not cover the range they asked about, say which dates are missing and answer for what you do have. Never estimate silently and never fill a gap.
5. Keep it under 80 words unless they asked for a table.

This is a lookup, not a report. Do not add sections, do not summarize the whole account, and do not turn it into a weekly roundup. When they want a full report, say that is not your job and offer the pace read or the latest check instead.

Nothing to save unless they ask you to keep the answer.

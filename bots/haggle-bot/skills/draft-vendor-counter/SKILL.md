# Draft a vendor counter

## Before drafting
Confirm the vendor row has a Focus, a Why, and a savings range with basis. If not, run score-savings-opportunity first.

## Structure
1. Lead with the commercial ask in one line.
2. Summary comparison table: current terms vs the option on the table vs our proposal.
3. The draft note.

## Voice rules (from memory, apply all)
- Conversational and abbreviated. Firm, not heavy-handed.
- Long-standing partners: friendly and collaborative, acknowledge the relationship in one clause, no bare imperatives.
- Email opens "Hi <name>," on its own line, blank line, then body. No em dash or hyphen after the greeting.
- Identify the sender as part of the operations team.
- Sign-off is "Best, <First name>" only. No email address in the signature.
- Never cite quote IDs or quote numbers. No "we've been through [quote] with finance."
- Slack notes to vendors use normal Slack form ("Hey <name>, ..." on one line), not the email greeting.
- One email only. No agent fingerprint.

## Send gate
This skill produces a draft. Sending it anywhere, including to another agent or a colleague for review, is a send and needs the operator's explicit go for that specific message. If unsure, it is not a go.

## Output
The table, the draft, and a one-line note on what evidence backs the ask.

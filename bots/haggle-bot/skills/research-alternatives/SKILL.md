Part of the Haggle Bot agent preset.

# Research alternatives

## Bar
- At least 3 named alternatives per line item, matched to how the company actually uses the tool, not the vendor's full feature list.
- Pricing from three angles: the vendor's public list page, a buyer benchmark source (Vendr, Tropic, Spendflo, or similar), and any real-buyer signal (forum posts, case studies, colleagues).
- Cite and date every number. Mark list vs street.
- Switching cost stated honestly: migration effort, integrations at risk, retraining, contract overlap.
- Quote-only vendors get a benchmark range with a confidence level, or an RFQ flag.

## Steps
1. Read the vendor's Purpose, Owner, and any usage notes. If usage is Unknown, ask the owner via a drafted DM (see the colleague DM rules in memory) before researching.
2. Shortlist candidates by the actual job, then verify each against the company's must-haves (SSO, data residency, integrations).
3. Build the comparison: current vendor vs each alternative on price basis, fit, switching cost, and risk.
4. Prefer email-only RFQs and skip live demos when a list price or written quote is enough.
5. Save the dossier to the vendor's row and the Switch tab.

## Output
Comparison table (current vs options), recommended path, savings range with basis and confidence, switching cost, and any RFQs to draft. Draft only.

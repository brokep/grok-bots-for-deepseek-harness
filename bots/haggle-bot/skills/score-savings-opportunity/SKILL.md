# score-savings-opportunity

Part of the Haggle Bot agent preset.

# Score a savings opportunity

## The bar (all three or it is a LEAD)
1. Dollars traced to live expense or ERP data, with the math shown.
2. A specific mechanism: Switch (replace the tool), Renegotiate (keep it, better terms), or Waste (cut residual seats or duplicate).
3. Why now: renewal inside the priority window, utilization evidence, or a competing quote in hand.

Waste claims need utilization evidence (seat reports, login data, or an owner confirming). Do not invent seat counts or amounts.

## Steps
1. Pull the vendor's row and the underlying Ramp charges and bills.
2. Check commitment: agreement, order form, or bill memo. Note term end and notice period.
3. Establish the basis for savings: public list pricing, a known cost at another company, or live expense data. State which.
4. Write the identified savings as a range with the basis. For Switch, call research-alternatives first.
5. Assign Focus and a one-line Why. If any bar item is missing, tag as LEAD with what is missing.
6. Add the vendor to the matching slim tab (Switch, Renegotiate, or Waste) with the $ range. Keep the Inventory row updated.
7. Run the Google Sheets final check.

## Output
One block: Vendor, current annual spend, Focus, mechanism, savings range and basis, why now, evidence gaps, next step. Polished for a procurement executive. No methodology wall.

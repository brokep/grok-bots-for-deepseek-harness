# Build the voice profile

Part of the Copy Humanizer agent preset.

Inputs: one to three pieces of writing the user likes (theirs or anyone's), a site or domain they name, or nothing at all.

1. Take what exists over what they can describe. Sent emails, a landing page, a post that did well, a competitor's page they envy. One piece is enough to start.
2. Given a URL or domain, read the public pages, usually the homepage, the about page, and a recent post. Quote the lines you are working from. Never research a company they did not name, and never take a brand claim, a customer name, or a number off a page and treat it as fact you can reuse.
3. Pull rules that can be checked, not adjectives. Typical sentence length and the longest they go. Contractions or none. I, we, or neither. How much they use "you". Headings in sentence case or title case. Punctuation habits, including dashes, exclamation marks, and emoji. How they open. How they close. What they call the product. Whether claims come with a number.
4. Write each rule with a quoted example under it. A rule you cannot show an example for gets cut.
5. Add a words-to-avoid list: the corporate words they already never use, plus the standing slop list from the anti-slop pass.
6. Show the profile in chat, take one round of edits, then save it.

Fallback with nothing to work from: use house style (short sentences, second person, contractions on, no hype, claims carry a number or get cut), say plainly that you are on house style, and build the real profile out of their edits to the first two drafts.

Keep the profile to 10 to 20 rules. Rewrite it, do not append to it, whenever their edits contradict a rule twice.

Save under copy desk as the voice profile.

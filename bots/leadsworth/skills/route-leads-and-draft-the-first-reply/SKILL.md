# Route leads and draft the first reply

Part of the Lead Pipeline Desk agent preset.

Inputs: the scored ledger, the routing rule from memory, and the response window.

1. Give an owner to every lead that has none, using the rule they gave you: one person, a round robin, by region, or by company size. Say which rule you applied.
2. Set a next step and a due date per tier. A gets a reply the same day, B this week, C goes to nurture with a date to look again. Anything past its window shows up as overdue.
3. Draft the first reply for the A leads. Three to five sentences in their voice, opening on the reason the lead came in, one specific line about what the person actually asked for, and one clear question. No pitch, no feature list.
4. Never invent a conversation that did not happen. When a form fill or a one-line note is all you have, say that is what you are working from.
5. Leave every draft unsent. When Gmail is connected, save it as a draft in their account and say it is sitting there unsent.
6. Write the owner, next step, due date, and draft status back to the ledger.

Output: the routing table with lead, owner, tier, next step, and due date, plus the drafts ready to copy.

When a lead has no email at all, route it anyway and say what is missing so someone can go find it. When two owners could take the same lead, pick by the rule and name the tie you broke.

Save drafts under the lead desk folder.

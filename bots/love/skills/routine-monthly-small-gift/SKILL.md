# Monthly small gift

Part of the Love ❤️ agent preset.

Schedule, as written by the template author: Monthly small gift

## What to do on each run

First of the month, 08:00. One small unprompted gift idea for his partner. One line to the owner with a link, under the monthly cap stored in the partner notes file.

Read the private partner notes file first so nothing repeats and it matches something she has said she likes (from him only). If no cap is set, ask him once for the amount and skip the idea until he answers.

Queue a product or basket link. Never buy. Never draft a card or note to his partner.

Read the private Life notes and act on any For Love lines. Write a short Life note for the day. Close with Worth your attention: yes + why / no. If no, five lines max.

Never put the partner notes file on the bus. Talk like a mate. No cheese.

Fires on:
- cron `0 8 1 * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

# Wednesday question

Part of the Love ❤️ agent preset.

Schedule, as written by the template author: Wednesday question

## What to do on each run

Wednesday 18:00. One line to the owner: a question he can ask his partner tonight that is about her, not logistics. Never "how was your day".

Read the private partner notes file first. Pull from what he has told you she mentioned, a thing she is working on, a person she is worried about, or a plan she floated. If the file is thin, use only a fact he has actually given. Do not invent a personal detail.

Read the private Life notes and act on any For Love lines. Write a short Life note for the day. Close with Worth your attention: yes + why / no. If no, five lines max.

Never message his partner. Never draft anything addressed to her. No relationship advice. One message only. Talk like a mate. No em dashes. No cheese.

# Interviewer prep packet

Part of the Recruiting Coordinator agent preset.

Inputs: the loop row, the job description, whatever the candidate sent (resume, portfolio, work samples), public professional profiles, and any earlier scorecards the user shared.

Steps:
1. One short page per interview, ordered by slot time.
2. Head each page with the slot: the time in both the candidate's and the user's timezone, the interviewer, and the one competency that interviewer owns, so two people do not ask the same thing.
3. Candidate summary in five lines or fewer, built only from what the user gave you and public professional work. Attach a source to every claim. Write "not stated" instead of filling a gap.
4. Four to six questions for that competency, plus the one follow-up that separates a real answer from a rehearsed one.
5. What earlier rounds already covered, and the open question this interviewer should close.
6. Logistics on one line: room or link, who greets the candidate, and when the scorecard is due.
7. Screen every question. Nothing about age, graduation year, family or pregnancy plans, health or disability, religion, national origin, or marital status. Work authorization only if their process requires it, and only as a yes or no. When a question they gave you drifts there, say so and offer a job-related version.

Output: the packets in chat, plus one line naming any slot with no interviewer or no competency assigned.

With no resume and no profile, build the packet from the job description and the role scorecard, and say the candidate summary is thin.

Save under the hiring folder with the date and the role in the filename.

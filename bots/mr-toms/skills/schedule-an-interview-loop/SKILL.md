# Schedule an interview loop

Part of the Recruiting Coordinator agent preset.

Inputs: the role and stage, the panel with who covers what and how long each slot runs, the candidate's availability windows with their timezone, interviewer constraints, and either Google Calendar or pasted availability.

Steps:
1. Confirm the shape before you look for time: how many slots, how long each one runs, what each one covers, and whether the panel needs gaps. A four-slot onsite needs 10 minutes between slots and one real break in the middle.
2. Write every window in both timezones, the candidate's and the user's, and label which is which. Never show a time without a timezone.
3. Find the overlaps. With Calendar connected, read the interviewers' free time. Otherwise work from what they pasted and say plainly that pasted availability is what you have.
4. Offer two or three complete loop options, best first. Each one gets the date, the slot times in both timezones, who takes which slot, and the single tradeoff: a tight gap, a backup interviewer, a late finish for someone.
5. On their pick, produce three things in one reply. The holds as a table they can copy, or drafted invites when Calendar is connected. A short candidate email with the times and what each conversation covers. A panel note telling each interviewer their slot and their competency.
6. Write the loop to the tracker with status confirmed or pending, and name anyone who has not accepted yet.

Reschedules and drop-outs keep the same competency coverage. When an interviewer falls out, name a replacement who covers that competency, or say the slot needs a new owner.

Never book, send, or invite anyone without an explicit yes for that specific action.

Save under the hiring folder.

# Humanizer

Use this final pass on emails, Slack or Teams drafts, meeting briefs, CRM notes, summaries, and agenda copy.

## Preserve meaning
- Keep every sourced fact, number, date, name, link, commitment, status, and uncertainty.
- Never add proof, customer names, activity, urgency, mutual connections, promises, or conclusions.
- Keep Unknown, Missing, and needs-input markers when the source does not answer something.
- A user-provided voice sample overrides the default style rules below.

## Default voice
- Lead with the useful point.
- Use plain words and direct verbs.
- Vary sentence length naturally.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Prefer concrete nouns over abstract business language.
- Match the installer's greeting, CTA, punctuation, and sign-off when real samples exist.
- Use straight quotes. Do not use em or en dashes.

## Remove
- Generic greetings and chatbot filler
- Inflated claims, sales language, and fake enthusiasm
- Repeated summaries and unnecessary headings
- Forced groups of three
- Vague attribution such as "experts say"
- Unsupported certainty
- Phrases such as "let's dive in," "at its core," "game-changing," and "I hope this helps"

## GTM safeguards
- Do not strengthen deal health, buyer intent, sentiment, forecast confidence, or objective progress beyond the source.
- Do not turn a meeting, email, or task into a completed milestone without confirmation.
- Do not write around a missing price, legal term, deadline, owner, or commitment. Mark it as needing input.
- Keep external communication and system updates in draft form until the user confirms the exact action.

## Return
Return only the cleaned draft unless the user asks for alternatives or an explanation.

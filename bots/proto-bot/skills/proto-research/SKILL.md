# proto-research

Craft research on X and the web: techniques, guides, prompts, and new models.

1. WebSearch for guides, docs, and examples, newest first, and WebFetch the best. On X: search_posts_all or search_news, else WebSearch for x.com posts and read them by link.
2. Keep only what has a source: link, author, date. Favor people who show results.
3. Write <home>/craft/<topic>.md: what works (steps, settings, prompt templates), what fails, models and costs, sources.
4. Send the file as an attachment with a 5-line summary, offer to try it on the user's own asset, and update the file when the topic comes back.

Never call a technique tested unless I ran it.

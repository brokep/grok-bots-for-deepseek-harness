# Next step repair

Part of the Sales Call Coach agent preset.

# Next step repair

Inputs: how the call ended, whatever was agreed, and how long it has been since.

## Steps
1. Grade the next step against four tests. Is there a date. Is there a time. Is it on both calendars. Does the buyer have something to do before it. Say which tests it failed.
2. Find the moment in the transcript where the close should have happened and quote it. It is usually the line right after the buyer said something positive.
3. Write what the rep should have said there, in their register, one or two sentences, ending in a specific ask.
4. Draft the follow-up. Subject line, then four to six sentences: what you heard in their words, what you agreed, what you owe them and by when, what they owe you, and one ask with a date and a time in it. No recap of your own pitch.
5. When the deal already went quiet, write the shorter version instead. One line naming the last thing they said they cared about, one line with a reason to talk now that is about them, one date ask.
6. Show the draft in chat. Send nothing. If they want it in Slack or Notion and those are connected, put it there after they say yes.

Output: the four-test verdict, the missed moment with the better line, and the follow-up draft.

Rules: never invent an agreement, a deadline, a name, or a commitment the buyer did not make. Never manufacture urgency. When nothing was agreed at all, say that first and write the re-open instead of pretending there is a thread to pull.

Save the draft dated in the coaching log.

# follow-through

## Week-later check-in

Customers only, calls 7 to 14 days ago with checkin empty: usage and spend in the 7 days after the call against the 7 before. Moved (up by the check-in change in setup.md, or first use of a feature from the talking points): one line. Not moved: a nudge per draft-email with one untried feature and why it fits what they build. Mark checkin.

## Wow moments

Customers with no wow moment date: check the wow moment metric in setup.md. First crossing: save the date and draft a congrats per draft-email with one way to go deeper and the offer under Incentives. Incentives none yet: ask the user once what to offer (credits they grant, a discount, swag, time with them, or nothing) and save it. The draft offers it, and only the user grants it.

## Feature series

Enroll on request: 3 untried features, step 0, next_due today, status active in series.csv. Due and active: draft the next per draft-email, add 1 to step, and set next_due 4 days out. After the last: status done. 3 declines in a row: status paused, with one line saying so.

## Monday impact read

Called: customers with a watched call in the last 8 weeks, weekly spend (or usage) change 4 weeks after the call against the 4 before. Others: the rest of the roster or sources, the same weeks around the median call date. Report each group's median change and count, the gap, the top 3 movers each way, and "Teams that book calls differ from teams that don't, so this is a signal, not proof." Under 5 called accounts: say it is too early, once. Never call a change caused by the call, or drop accounts to flatter a group.

# prep-page

## Steps

1. The event and the account file, with outreach facts for a booked prospect.
2. Learnings, open asks in requests.csv, and recent drafts.
3. Numbers per accounts: 30 days of usage and spend with the week-over-week change, credits and runway, plan, features used. Prospects: skip.
4. Research: their company and product in two lines, the people on the invite with roles, new posts from saved X handles.
5. Site walk, for prospects and first calls unless Site walk in setup.md says otherwise: one pass of their homepage and signup in my browser, and a screenshot of the most broken thing.
6. Product walk: the competitor-intel teammate's notes in the account file. A first call without them, and the teammate exists: ask it for a walk per accounts, for next time.

## Page

prep/<date>-<slug>.html, self-contained (inline CSS, screenshots as data URIs):
- Who: people with roles, company in one line, stage and time with you.
- Numbers, each labeled.
- Not tried yet: 3 features with the most upside for what they build.
- Since last time: learnings, open asks, fixes shipped.
- Talking points: 3, each tied to a number or a note.
- The one question to ask, and the risks.
- Could not read.
Attach it with 5 lines: who and when, the headline number, the feature to show, the question, the biggest risk. Mark prep in calls.csv.

## Confirm note

About an hour before, per draft-email: 2 or 3 lines confirming the time and link and asking what they most want to cover. Skip it when they confirmed in the last day or the event is canceled. Mark confirm. Never invent a feature use or a role.

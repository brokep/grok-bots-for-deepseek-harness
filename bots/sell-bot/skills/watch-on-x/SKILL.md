# watch-on-x

Prospects, customers, and watched X accounts. Read only: I cannot follow, like, reply, or post.

## Find handles

Their site footer, docs, blog, and email signatures first, then X search for the company and each contact plus the company. Score: linked from their domain, bio names the company, posts about their work. Drop parody, fan, support-only, and regional accounts, and any silent 180 days. One company handle and up to two people. No handle beats a wrong one.

## Confirm

In chat, one widget: each candidate with bio, followers, last post date, and evidence, pre-checking only handles linked from their domain. Options: Watch these (primary), Edit, Skip X for this account. In a routine: only domain-linked handles, listed in the report. Save confirmed handles in the account file with the date, and give the user follow links: "Follow @handle: https://x.com/handle".

## Watch

Posts newer than the last post id per handle, about $0.005 a post: daily for customers, watched X accounts, and prospects in replied, call booked, proposal, or contract, weekly for other prospects. Flag posts about the user's product, their market, the problem it solves, a launch, funding, hiring, or a complaint, one line each with the link. A prospect's flag is the reason for the next touch. Update the X line under Last checks. Off: skip X silently. Never watch a handle the user unchecked.

# Answer engine question map

Part of the SEO & AEO Desk agent preset.

Inputs: their site, what they sell, their audience, the ideas board, and any prompt-visibility export they already have.

1. Build the question list. 20 to 40 questions a real buyer would type into an assistant, grouped into what is this, how do I choose, how does it compare, how do I do it, and is it worth it.
2. Ask the questions yourself where you can. Note what a good answer looks like, which sources keep getting cited, and whether the user's site is one of them.
3. Map each question to what they have today: covered well, covered badly, or nothing at all.
4. Mark the gaps worth a page. A gap is worth it when the question sits close to buying and the current answers are thin or wrong about their category.
5. Say what makes a page quotable: the direct answer in the first paragraph, plain numbers, a clear definition, and a table an assistant can lift.

Output: the question map with one row per question (question, stage, what they have, the page that should answer it, priority), the 5 gaps to fix first, and an offer to brief the top one.

Fallbacks: Profound, Semrush, Ahrefs, or any prompt-visibility tool they already pay for adds real citation data the moment they paste an export. None of them is required and you never suggest they buy one. Without an export, say your read comes from asking the questions directly, so it is a sample rather than a measurement.

Save the question map under the search content folder.

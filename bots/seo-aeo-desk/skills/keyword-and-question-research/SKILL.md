# Keyword and question research

Part of the SEO & AEO Desk agent preset.

Inputs: a site on its own, pasted keywords, a CSV or export from any keyword tool, a Search Console query export, their competitors, or just the category they sell into.

1. Start from whatever they gave you. With nothing but a site, read their pages, pull the terms they already write about, read the competitors they named, and expand from there. Never send them away to go get keywords first.
2. Expand every seed three ways: what people type into a search box, the questions they ask an assistant in full sentences, and the comparison and alternative terms buyers use late.
3. Check what already exists. Search each cluster and note who ranks, what the top pages actually cover, and whether the user already has a page on it.
4. Group into clusters of 3 to 8 terms with one page idea each. A cluster is one brief, not one term per brief.
5. Rank each idea on three things you can defend: how close the term is to buying, how thin the current top results are, and whether the user has something real to say. Never invent a search volume, a difficulty score, or a position. When a number comes from their export, name the export and its date.
6. Write the rows to the ideas board: cluster, page idea, intent, terms, who ranks now, status, source, date. Status is idea, queued, briefed, or published.

Output: the top 5 to 10 ideas in chat with one line each on why, then the full board saved. Offer a brief on the top pick in the same message.

Fallbacks: no export and no connected tool means you work from public search results and their own site, and you say plainly which numbers you do not have. A CSV that will not parse means you ask them to paste the rows.

Save the ideas board under the search content folder.

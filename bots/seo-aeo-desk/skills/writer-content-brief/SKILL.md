# Writer content brief

Part of the SEO & AEO Desk agent preset.

Inputs: the topic or cluster, its row on the ideas board, their audience and what they sell, and the pages that rank today.

1. Confirm the target in one line: the page idea, the primary term, and who it is for. Ask only when the ideas board does not already say.
2. Read the top 5 results and the answer an assistant gives for the main question. Note what all of them cover, and what none of them do.
3. Write the brief:
   - Angle. One or two sentences on the take that makes this page worth reading.
   - Reader and moment. Who lands here and what they need next.
   - Primary term, supporting terms, and the questions the page has to answer outright.
   - Outline. H2 and H3 with a line under each on what belongs there.
   - Proof. The data, examples, screenshots, or customer lines the writer needs, and who to get them from.
   - Internal links in and out, with the URLs.
   - Two title options and two meta description options.
   - Word count range, and the one thing that would make this the best result on the page.
4. Add an answer-ready block: the two or three questions this page should answer in a single quotable paragraph, so an assistant can lift it.
5. Do not write the finished article unless they ask for it outright. The brief is the deliverable.

Output: the brief in chat, saved dated, and sent to the destination they picked, whether that is a doc, a workspace page, or a ticket on their content backlog. Mark the ideas board row briefed.

Fallback: no ideas board row means you build the brief from the topic alone and say which parts are your read rather than their research.

Save briefs under the search content folder.

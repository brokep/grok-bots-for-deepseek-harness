# Draft outreach

Part of the Talent Discovery agent preset.

Inputs: the candidate card, the scorecard, who the message comes from, and the channel.

1. One message per person. Never a template with the name swapped in.
2. Shape, 4 to 6 sentences. Open with the specific thing you found and where you found it. One line on the role and why this person in particular. One line on what they would actually care about, the problem, the scope, the team, or the stage. One clear ask as a question, on its own line.
3. Say where you found them. It is the most credible line in the message and it is the honest thing to do.
4. Facts only from the card. Never invent a mutual connection, never claim you read something you did not, and never write "I came across your profile" when you can name the talk, the repo, or the writeup.
5. No hype. No "exciting opportunity", no "rockstar", no flattery the user would not say out loud. Match how the user writes when you have seen their writing.
6. Pay, title, and start date only when the user gave them to you. Never estimate a band.
7. Follow-up: one, after 5 business days, three sentences, a new angle instead of "just bumping this". Stop after two touches unless the user says otherwise, and record a no the first time you hear it.
8. Everything is a draft. Never send, message, or post. With Gmail connected, leave it unsent in their drafts and say it is sitting there.

Log the draft against the candidate with the date and the channel, so the weekly recap can tell who is waiting on a reply.

Save under the search folder.

# Role market read

Part of the Talent Discovery agent preset.

Inputs: the scorecard, the batches sourced so far, and public job postings for the same role.

1. Say up front that this is a read off public information and a small sample, not a market report.
2. Who else is hiring the same person right now. Count live postings at comparable companies and name the five most likely to compete for the same shortlist, with links.
3. Where the people cluster. The company types, the cities, and the communities that produced the strongest cards so far, with counts from your own batches.
4. Pay, only from postings and public pay data that state a number, each with the link. Never estimate a band and never infer one from a company's stage.
5. Bar pressure. For each must-have, say roughly how many of the people you screened cleared it. Name the one or two constraints costing the most, usually location, one must-have, or pay, and state the trade-off in plain terms: loosen this and the pool roughly doubles.
6. A realistic read on how long it takes to get a shortlist of the size they want, based on the hit rate you are actually seeing.
7. One recommendation, one line, on the single change that helps most.

Too few batches to say anything real? Say that and give the read after the next one instead of guessing.

Save under the search folder.

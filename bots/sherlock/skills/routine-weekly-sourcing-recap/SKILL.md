# Weekly sourcing recap

Part of the Talent Discovery agent preset.

Schedule, as written by the template author: Every Friday, a recap of who was added, what you kept and passed on, who has not replied yet, and whether the bar needs to move.

## What to do on each run

Run every Friday at the hour the user picked, in the timezone saved in memory. Compute the cron from that timezone. Read the shortlist, the outreach log, and the scorecard, then write five short parts. Added this week, the count plus the three strongest names with one evidence line and a link each. Decisions, how many were kept and passed, and the two most common pass reasons in the user's own words. Waiting on a reply, everyone contacted more than five business days ago with the date and the channel, and the follow-up drafts ready to go. Bar check, one line on whether those pass reasons mean the scorecard needs an edit, with the specific change you would make. Next week, where you plan to look and what you need from the user to get further. A week with nothing logged gets two lines, what is still open and the one thing to do first. Never invent a candidate, a reply, or a number, and never contact anyone from this run.

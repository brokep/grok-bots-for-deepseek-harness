# Share the shortlist

Part of the Talent Discovery agent preset.

Inputs: the shortlist, the scorecard, and where it should go.

1. Ask what goes out: the whole shortlist, the strong fits only, or one card. Confirm the destination before you write anything.
2. Build the summary in this shape. The role and the bar in one line each, then the counts, then one row per candidate: name, current title and company, the strongest piece of evidence with its link, and the status.
3. Destinations, whichever is connected. Notion, a page with the bar and the shortlist as a table, updated in place on later runs. Slack, a short message to the channel they name, the top three with links and a line on the rest. Linear, one issue for the search or one per candidate when the team tracks hiring there, with the evidence in the body. Google Sheets, one row per candidate with the same columns.
4. No connector, no problem. Hand back a clean block they can paste anywhere, or a CSV file.
5. Never include anyone marked do not contact, and never include a private note the user asked you to keep between the two of you.
6. On a repeat share, update the existing page, sheet, or issue instead of posting a second copy, and say what changed since last time.
7. Report where it went and what is in it, in one line.

Save under the search folder.

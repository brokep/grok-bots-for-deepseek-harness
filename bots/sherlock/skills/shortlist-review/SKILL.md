# Shortlist review

Part of the Talent Discovery agent preset.

Inputs: the shortlist, the scorecard, and whatever the user says about each name.

1. Show five at a time, strongest first. One line each: name, current title and company, the single strongest piece of evidence, and the link.
2. Take a keep, a pass, or a maybe on each. Ask for the reason in a few words on every pass. The reason is the whole point of this.
3. Write the decision and the reason onto the shortlist, dated.
4. Watch for the pattern. Three passes for the same reason means the bar is wrong, so say it plainly and propose the exact edit: "You have passed on three people for agency-only backgrounds, want me to make in-house product work a must-have?"
5. Apply the edit only on a yes, then re-rank what is left and say what moved.
6. Report the funnel in four numbers: sourced, kept, passed, contacted. Add the top two pass reasons underneath.
7. Close on what you will do next, not a question about whether that was useful.

An empty shortlist gets one line saying so and an offer to source a batch instead.

Save under the search folder.

# first-touch-drafts

## When to use

After `contact-ranking`, for the contacts the owner wants to touch this week. Input: the ranked contact list with hooks.

## Shape

Sixty to 110 words. Hook, bridge, soft CTA. Short paragraphs, no bullets, no feature list, greeting line first, the owner's sign-off last.

- Hook: the strongest signal for this contact, stated specifically. A team signal ("nine engineers at Acme created accounts last month") beats a company signal ("Acme is hiring"). A signup is evidence in a message to their manager, not an accusation to the person who signed up.
- Bridge: why that signal makes what we sell relevant to this persona's KPI, in one or two sentences. One approved proof point at most, only if it fits.
- CTA: one casual, low-friction question.

## Voice

Read the owner's voice profile if one exists (greeting, sign-off, sentence length, signature). Without one, write plainly and ask the owner for two sample emails to learn from.

## Quality pass

Remove any unverified metric, logo, or claim about their internal usage that the data does not support. Remove assumed departments or regions. No em dashes, no hyperbole. If the hook does not survive the pass, drop to the next signal or skip the contact and say why.

## Deliver

Save each draft to Drafts addressed to the verified email, subject under six words. Show the owner a table: Contact, Hook used, Draft link, Confidence. Contacts without a verified email get a draft with no recipient and a note.

## Rules

- Drafts only. Never sends, never enrolls in a sequence, never sends LinkedIn messages.
- Skip anyone in an open opportunity owned by someone else.

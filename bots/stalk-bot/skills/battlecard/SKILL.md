# Battlecard

## Sections
Who they are (their words), who they sell to, pricing as of <date>, where they win (with evidence), where we win (with evidence from baseline and teardown), likely objections a prospect will raise and a factual response for each, landmines (claims they make that we can disprove, with links), recent moves (last 30 days from pulses), open questions.

## Rules
Every claim about them has a link or screenshot date. Every claim about us comes from the baseline or the user. No adjectives without a number or example behind them. Draft only: write to /workspace/dossiers/<competitor>/battlecard.md, summarize in chat, and if a writing or GTM messaging bot exists on the team, offer to send it over with SendToAgent.

## Also include
- Website pros / cons (from `site-pros-cons.md`, dated)
- **For our site**: 2 to 3 concrete changes to the user's own website or pricing page that would blunt their wins (questions or draft copy directions, not published copy unless asked)

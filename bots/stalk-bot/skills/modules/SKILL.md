# Modules

## Gameplan first
During setup present modules as a recommended gameplan tied to the company category, not a blank checklist. Time to first value: spinning a research inbox and returning a first-seen competitor finding beats collecting every preference. MODULES the user can turn on per competitor. (1) Newsletter pulse: subscribe under a research identity, read every issue, track positioning language. (2) Product walkthrough: sign up, log in, walk onboarding on video, then aggressively test every reachable feature with test data (happy path, empty state, limits, invalid input, plan gates, integrations, exports, settings), a clip and screenshots per feature, ending in a self-contained HTML teardown report. For open-source or downloadable products: clone or install on my computer and run it, browser first if it is a web product, install path if it is not, and if install fails tell the user plainly and fall back to the browser or public materials. (3) Website watch: snapshot and diff home, about, docs index, careers. (4) Pricing watch: snapshot and diff the pricing page, dated before and after. (5) Changelog tracker: parse their changelog or what's new, dated entries, velocity vs ours. (6) X radar: competitor, product, and founder handles plus brand mentions via the X connector. Builds a dated shipped timeline per competitor cross-referenced with their changelog, tracks positioning shifts and engagement. Read-only, never follows or posts. (7) Complaint mining: X replies and mentions where users complain, grouped by theme. (8) Open roles: careers page and hosted job board diff, new and closed roles by function, a 2 to 4 line direction read of where they are investing. Screenshot each new posting. (9) Sequence anatomy: book a demo or start a trial under the research identity, let their sales sequence run, document every email and its ask. Read-only. I never reply to competitor marketing or sales emails unless the user explicitly instructs a specific reply. (10) Battlecard: dossier to battlecard on demand, draft only. (11) Feature matrix: features by competitor, refreshed from dossiers, changed cells highlighted. (12) Positioning drift: hero and subhead timeline. (13) Competitor churn winback: opt-in, needs a churn source the user names (CRM closed-lost or competitor field, a sheet, a paste). Detects the user's customers lost to a watched competitor, attributes, writes a facts brief, hands the winback body to the user's writing bot or a marked skeleton. Rules in competitor-churn. Defaults: 1 newsletter pulse, 2 product walkthrough (wherever a card-free free tier or trial exists), 3 website watch, 4 pricing watch, 5 changelog tracker, 6 X radar, 8 open roles, and 12 positioning drift are on for every competitor. 13 competitor churn winback is opt-in and stays off until a churn source exists. 9 sequence anatomy is opt-in only because it puts the identity into their sales pipeline. "All" means everything except 9.

## Time to first value
The setup succeeds when the user has (a) an editable competitor shortlist they did not have to invent, (b) a clear gameplan they can accept in one tap, and (c) a live research inbox address plus a first Against-us finding (with Website pros/cons and at least one For-our-site next step) within minutes of naming their company. Optionality stays available (tweak modules, skip inboxes, add competitors) but never blocks the test drive.

## Known limits (say them plainly when relevant)
say them plainly when relevant. (1) Newsletter and sequence mail is read on each pulse, not the moment it arrives. (2) Product walkthroughs need a free tier or card-free trial. Card, phone, ID, SSO-only, or terms forbidding competitive use stop that module and become findings. (3) The X modules need the X plugin connected. Without it I skip them and say so once. (4) Site diffs are text-based. Image-only changes and changes behind a login I do not have are not detected. (5) Open-source products may not build on my Linux computer. When that happens I say exactly what failed and evaluate public materials instead. (6) Prefer the AgentMail marketplace plugin for research inboxes. If it is not connected I fall back to the AgentMail REST API with a key stored as a secret (env AGENTMAIL_API_KEY), a teammate identity bot, or Gmail.

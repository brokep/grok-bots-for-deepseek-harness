# What moved and why

Part of the Paid Media Report Desk agent preset.

Inputs: the current and prior period figures, the campaign level rows, and anything the user has told you about launches, budget changes, or seasonality.

1. Start from the biggest change in spend, then the biggest change in CPA, then ROAS when you have conversion value.
2. Decompose each move with the columns you actually have. A CPA move is a CPC move or a conversion rate move. A CPC move is a CTR move or a CPM move. Say which one carries the change and how much of it.
3. Tie it to something visible in the data: a campaign that started or stopped, a budget shift you can see in daily spend, a new ad set, a large impression swing. Name the campaign.
4. When the numbers cannot explain the move, say that in one line and name the one thing you would need to know. Never reach for seasonality, algorithm changes, or market conditions as filler.
5. Call a thin sample what it is. Fewer than about 30 conversions on a platform in the period gets a line saying the CPA move sits inside the noise.
6. Keep it to three to five lines for a week, four to six for a month. Each line: what happened, the number, and what you would do about it.

Rules: no claim without a number behind it, and no recommendation to raise or cut a budget without the metric that justifies it. Never compare one platform's attributed conversions to another platform's unless the user has confirmed the windows match. Never estimate a figure that is missing from the export.

The commentary belongs inside the report, so hand it back to the report skill instead of sending it on its own.

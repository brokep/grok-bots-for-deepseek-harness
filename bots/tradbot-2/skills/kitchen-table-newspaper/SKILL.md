# Kitchen table newspaper

Use this when the household wants a printable one-page morning newspaper for the kitchen table.

## Output

Generate a single-page PDF, attach it in chat, and add one short line that the paper is ready to print. Do not dump the full newspaper as chat text.

## Fonts (locked — do not switch)

- Headlines and body: Liberation Serif / Georgia
- Kickers, weather, schedule labels: Nunito
- Never introduce a third typeface. Do not use Fredoka. Cute weather is layout and doodles, not a font change.

## Layout (do not invert)

US Letter, portrait, white page. Classic newspaper: masthead, column rules, fill the whole page. Tight margins. Not cute cards.

**Schedule at the top.** A compact key-events strip under the date line (today, tonight, weekend, coming up). Only the real ones.

**Weather is the one cute section.** Full-width strip under the schedule: sky doodle, kid-voice one-liner, big high/low, pack chips. Same fonts as the rest. Do not make the rest of the paper cutesy.

**Left column (wider): family.** This is a whole column, not a footer box.
- Today's school day
- The kids (tests, folders, sports news they can read)
- This weekend at our house
- Smaller parent don't-forgets at the bottom of that column

**Two smaller right columns: the outside world.**
- 4–6 real kid-appropriate stories (science, space, animals, sports, local)
- Look them up. Do not invent. One-line source is enough.
- This weekend / around town if something fun and real

Skip crime, war, politics, medical scares.

## Leave off the page

Therapy, medical details, money stress, work meetings, home address.

## Design

White page. Friendly, not corporate. One page, readable from a few feet. Columns must reach the footer. If it spills to page 2, tighten; if the bottom is empty, add a short real story. When revising, change only what was asked. Do not restyle the whole paper.

A quiet household day still gets the page (weather plus real news). Never invent a family item to fill a section. Never send mail, RSVP, or change the calendar from this skill.

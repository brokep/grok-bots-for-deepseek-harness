Use this for a once-a-month household note: upcoming local events worth considering, plus age-adjusted development and parenting ideas. Not the weekday digest and not the kitchen newspaper.

## Output

One short chat note. A handful of bullets, not a magazine. Attach nothing unless a flyer or calendar PDF is actually useful.

## Local events

Look up real events in the household's city for the next 4–6 weeks.
- Family-friendly: museums, parks, festivals, sports, libraries, free or low-key outings
- Match ages on the watch list (a 2nd grader and a 4th grader get different picks than a toddler)
- Only real, dated events. Do not invent. One-line source is enough.
- Skip crime, war, politics, medical scares, and anything that needs a home address

## Age-adjusted help

Using each kid's age and grade from the watch list (never guess a birth year):
- One or two development notes that actually fit that age (independence, homework, sports, friendships, sleep)
- One practical parenting tactic per age band, not a lecture
- Use established, kid-appropriate guidance. Do not invent milestones or medical advice. Skip therapy, diagnoses, and scary health content.

## Shape

1. This month — 3–5 local events with date, who it fits, and whether you need to register
2. For the kids — one short note per child, named the way the parent names them
3. For you — one or two parent tactics that match the household as it is now

A quiet month still sends: one event if you found one, plus the age notes. Never pad with fake events.

## Leave off

Home address, medical details, money stress, work meetings. Never send, RSVP, or add a calendar event from this run unless the user asked.

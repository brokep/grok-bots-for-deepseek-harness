# Strip AI-isms

Part of the Webby agent preset.

Use when writing or rewriting the owner's copy.

## Field guide
Primary reference: [Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing)

## Kill list (high-signal)
- Puff / significance filler: pivotal, testament, underscore, delve, tapestry, landscape (abstract), vibrant, robust, meticulous, fostering, showcasing, indelible mark, evolving landscape
- Copula avoidance: “serves as / stands as / marks a” instead of “is”
- Negative parallelisms: “not only X, but Y”; “it’s not X, it’s Y”
- Em-dash spam (especially space-padded clauses)
- Boldface “key takeaways” / inline-header bullet lists
- Rule-of-three adjective stacks and generic triad filler
- Weasel: “experts argue”, “observers note”, “broader debates”
- Collaborative chatbot residue: “I hope this helps”, “Certainly!”, knowledge-cutoff disclaimers

## Target voice
Urgent explainer with stakes. Concrete nouns. Specific numbers/names when known. Short sentences. Use the owner's display name, not a full legal name unless legally required.

## Process
1. Draft or load the copy.
2. Cut or rewrite every AI tell; prefer deletion over synonym swaps that keep the same filler structure.
3. Read aloud test: would the owner say this on camera?
4. Do not “AI-wash” by only swapping buzzwords while keeping hollow structure.

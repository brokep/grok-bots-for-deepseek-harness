# Comms source of truth

Apply whenever adding information into a deck, plan, tracker, or similar project deliverable.

## Always review (all three)
1. **Email** — most accurate for dates, pricing, commitments, partner replies. Prefer latest message in the relevant thread.
2. **Slack** — decisions, internal alignment, open asks.
3. **Notion** — meeting notes, goals, private planning notes.

## Conflict rule
- If sources disagree, **email wins** for factual commitments (dates, dollars, seats, contract language).
- Use Slack/Notion for context, framing, and open questions — call out conflicts explicitly in Done vs Outstanding.

## Deliver
- Short overview of what each source said (1–3 bullets each) when the fact is material.
- Then put the email-backed fact into the deck/project.
- Never invent dates or invent partner commitments not in email.

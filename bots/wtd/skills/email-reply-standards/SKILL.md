# Email reply standards

Apply whenever drafting, proposing, or sending email as the owner.

## Always (unless the owner specifies otherwise)
1. **Reply to the latest message** in the thread — not an older message higher up.
2. **Reply All** when there are multiple people on the thread (To + Cc). Use Reply only when the owner explicitly says so, or when they are the sole recipient and a one-to-one reply is clearly intended.

## Still always
- Never send external email without the owner's explicit OK.
- Match their email tone (usually more formal than Slack).

# Draft a post or reply

Part of the X Brief agent preset.

Inputs: the item or thread they picked, their beat, and their own past posts when they have shared any.

1. Ask nothing you can infer. Their angle is the only thing worth asking about, and only when the item allows more than one.
2. Match how they write. With past posts in hand, keep their sentence length, casing, and vocabulary. Without them, write plain and short and say you guessed at the voice.
3. Give three drafts in different shapes: the flat statement, the one with a specific number or example, and the question. Each under 280 characters unless they asked for a thread.
4. A thread gets a first post that stands on its own, then three to six posts of one point each, and a last post with the link.
5. A reply stays under two sentences and adds something the original post did not say.
6. Attribute anything you took from someone else's post, with the link.
7. Never claim a number, a customer, or a result the user has not given you. Never write engagement bait, and never write a subtweet about a named person.

Output: the drafts in chat ready to copy, each with its source link under it, and one line saying nothing has been sent.

Never post, reply, like, or DM, even when the user asked for the draft. Sending is a separate yes for that specific message.

Save drafts dated under x-brief.

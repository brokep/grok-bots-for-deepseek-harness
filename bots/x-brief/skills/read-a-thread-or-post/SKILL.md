# Read a thread or post

Part of the X Brief agent preset.

Inputs: one or more links, or a screenshot.

1. Open the link and read the whole thread, including the author's own replies underneath it. Say plainly when the page will not open.
2. Write it in three parts: the claim in one line, the support they give in two to four lines, and what is missing or unproven.
3. Add one line on the author: who they are, and why they would or would not know.
4. Check any number, benchmark, or quote against a second source before you repeat it. Label anything you could not verify as unverified.
5. Pull up to three replies worth reading, each with a link and a clause on why.
6. Asked whether it is true, answer with what you can confirm and what you cannot. Never fill the gap with a guess.

Output: the three-part read, the author line, the replies, and the source link at the top.

Fallbacks: a page that will not open means you say so, then search the public web for the post being quoted or written up elsewhere. Never reconstruct wording you did not read. A screenshot is a claim about a post, so treat it as one and say the source is a screenshot.

Save under x-brief when the user wants to keep it.

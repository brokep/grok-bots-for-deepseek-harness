# Weekly X recap

Part of the X Brief agent preset.

Inputs: the dated briefs from the last seven days, the watch list, and the seen log.

1. Read the week's briefs instead of refetching the week, then top up with anything posted since the last brief.
2. Open with one line: the date range and how many items you logged.
3. Themes that held. Two to four, each with the two or three posts that carried it, a link on every line, and one clause on which way the argument moved.
4. New voices. Accounts that showed up in your beat more than once this week and are not on the watch list, one line on who they are, and an offer to add them.
5. Went quiet. The accounts on the list with nothing all week, so the user can drop them.
6. Still open. The questions the field is arguing about with no answer yet.
7. Read this weekend. Up to five links, threads, long posts, and linked writing, one clause each on why.

Rules: reuse what you already logged rather than re-reading the week from scratch. Never pad a thin week into a full recap. A week with almost nothing gets three lines.

Fallbacks: a week with no saved briefs means you say so and build the recap from a fresh read of the watch list over seven days, which is slower, so tell them it is running.

Save the recap dated under x-brief and attach it here, then deliver it to the destination they picked.
